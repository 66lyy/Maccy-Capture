## English

Maccy Capture 2.11.0 is a stable release of the unofficial fork of [p0deje/Maccy](https://github.com/p0deje/Maccy).

### Changes

- Long screenshots match overlapping frames more reliably when scrollbars or floating toolbars change, preserve more content at joins, and prompt you to slow down when frames cannot be matched.
- The region selection magnifier shows the color of the pixel under the pointer. Press C to copy the hex value or Shift+C to copy the RGB value.
- Clipboard history search can find screenshots by the text recognized in them, even when the text is not in the item's title.
- Pinned screenshots can let clicks pass through to the app underneath. A dashed outline and corner badge mark click-through pins. Use the history toolbar to turn click-through off.

### Download and install

- Requires macOS 14 or later. The same ZIP and DMG support Apple silicon and Intel Macs.
- Download either package, extract or open it, then move `Maccy Capture.app` to Applications. If you already use Maccy Capture, check for updates on the Stable channel.
- Verify downloads with `SHA256SUMS.txt`. `BUILD-INFO.txt` records the build details.
- The app is signed with a fixed self-signed certificate, not Developer ID signed or Apple-notarized. macOS may block the first launch. Updating from a build with a different signature may require granting screen recording and Accessibility permission again. The update ZIP has a separate Ed25519 signature.

## 简体中文

Maccy Capture 2.11.0 是基于 [p0deje/Maccy](https://github.com/p0deje/Maccy) 的非官方修改版的稳定版本。

### 更新内容

- 长截图在滚动条或悬浮工具栏变化时能更稳定地匹配重叠帧，减少拼接处的内容丢失；无法匹配时会提示放慢滚动速度。
- 区域截图的放大镜会显示指针所在像素的颜色。按 C 复制十六进制色值，按 Shift+C 复制 RGB 色值。
- 剪贴板历史搜索可通过截图中识别出的文字找到截图，即使这些文字不在条目标题中。
- 贴屏截图可开启点击穿透，让点击传到下方的应用。开启后会显示虚线边框和角标，可在历史窗口的工具栏中关闭点击穿透。

### 下载与安装

- 支持 macOS 14 或更高版本。ZIP 和 DMG 均为通用包，适用于 Apple 芯片和 Intel Mac。
- 下载任一安装包，解压或打开后将 `Maccy Capture.app` 移到「应用程序」。如果已安装，可在应用内选择「稳定版」并检查更新。
- 使用 `SHA256SUMS.txt` 校验下载；`BUILD-INFO.txt` 记录构建信息。
- 应用使用固定的自签名证书签名，未使用 Developer ID 签名，也未通过 Apple 公证。macOS 可能拦截首次启动。从签名不同的版本更新后，可能需要重新授予屏幕录制和辅助功能权限。更新 ZIP 有独立的 Ed25519 签名。
